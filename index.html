FAQ

Common questions

Where is Atlantis Three Sixty located?

Atlantis Three Sixty is located on PR-7, Chandigarh International Airport Road, Zirakpur, Punjab 140603 — close to the Zirakpur bus stand, railway station and Chandigarh International Airport.

What configurations are available?

3 BHK (Towers B & C), 3+1 BHK with servant room (Towers A & E), and 4+1 BHK with servant room (Tower D), plus limited 4+1 BHK penthouses in Towers A & E.

Is Atlantis Three Sixty RERA registered?

Yes, the project is registered under RERA Punjab with registration number PBRERA-SAS79-PR1159.

Who is developing Atlantis Three Sixty?

The project is developed by Krishna Buildtech, which has also delivered Atlantis Grand and Atlantis Heights in Zirakpur.

How many towers and units does the project have?

5 towers spread across 4.75 acres, comprising only 178 residences in total — making it one of the lowest-density projects in the Tricity region.

What is the starting price?

Prices start from approximately ₹1.88 Cr onwards, varying by tower, floor and configuration. Share your requirement using the enquiry form for current pricing.

What amenities are included?

A clubhouse, swimming pool, gymnasium, banquet hall, basketball and cricket courts, kids' play area, jogging/cycling track, yoga zone, spa, library and senior citizen relaxation zone.

Does every apartment get cross-ventilation?

Yes — each floor has only two apartments (dual-core layout), and residences are designed open on three sides with wrap-around balconies for daylight and airflow on multiple sides.
