Location & Connectivity

Everything Close on PR-7 Airport Road, Zirakpur — Nothing Crowded

Atlantis Three Sixty sits directly on PR-7, Chandigarh International Airport Road — one of the fastest-developing addresses in Tricity. It's a location built for a specific kind of convenience: close enough to reach the city, the airport and everyday essentials in minutes, but set back enough to avoid the noise and congestion that denser parts of Zirakpur can't escape.

On PR-7, Chandigarh International Airport Road

PR-7 has become the connectivity spine for this part of Zirakpur, linking the town directly to Chandigarh International Airport in one direction and the heart of Tricity in the other. For residents, that means a daily commute measured in minutes, not traffic jams — and a location that's likely to keep gaining value as the road corridor develops further.

Why This Address Matters

Most "location advantage" claims in Zirakpur real estate boil down to proximity alone. Atlantis Three Sixty pairs that proximity with something rarer on PR-7: a low-density, IGBC Platinum–certified project on 4.75 acres, where being close to everything doesn't mean living on top of everyone else.
