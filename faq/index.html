FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything buyers ask us about Atlantis Three Sixty — RERA registration, configurations, pricing, construction and what's actually included.

Where is Atlantis Three Sixty located?

Atlantis Three Sixty is located on PR-7, Chandigarh International Airport Road, Zirakpur, Punjab 140603 — close to the Zirakpur bus stand, railway station and Chandigarh International Airport. See the full location & connectivity guide.

What configurations are available?

3 BHK (Towers B & C), 3+1 BHK with servant room (Towers A & E), and 4+1 BHK with servant room (Tower D), including penthouses in Towers A & E.

Is Atlantis Three Sixty RERA registered?

Yes. The project is registered under RERA Punjab with registration number PBRERA-SAS79-PR1159. You can verify this directly on the RERA Punjab website.

Who is developing Atlantis Three Sixty?

The project is developed by Krishna Buildtech, which has also delivered Atlantis Grand and Atlantis Heights.

How many towers and units does the project have?

5 towers spread across 4.75 acres, comprising only 178 residences in total — making it one of the lowest-density projects on PR-7.

What is the starting price?

Prices start from approximately ₹1.88 Cr onwards, varying by tower, floor and configuration. Share your requirement for the current price sheet.

What amenities are included?

A clubhouse, swimming pool, gymnasium, banquet hall, basketball and cricket courts, kids' play area, jogging and cycling track, yoga and meditation zone, spa and wellness center, library, outdoor seating, and a senior citizen relaxation zone.

Does every apartment get cross-ventilation?

Yes — each floor has only two apartments (dual-core layout), and residences are designed open on three sides.

What is Mivan construction, and why does it matter?

Atlantis Three Sixty is built using Mivan shuttering technology, a monolithic construction method that produces stronger, more seamless concrete structures with faster, more precise execution than conventional construction.

Who are the architects and landscape designers?

Architecture is led by Ar. Tripat & Subah & Associates, with landscape design by Oracle Landscape — a dedicated design consortium rather than a single in-house team.

How many apartments are there per floor?

Each tower is built around a dual-core plan with only two homes per floor, both open on three sides.

What's the difference between 3+1 BHK and 4+1 BHK?

The "+1" typically refers to an added servant room. See our full 3+1 BHK vs 4+1 BHK guide for a detailed breakdown of which configuration suits different family needs.
