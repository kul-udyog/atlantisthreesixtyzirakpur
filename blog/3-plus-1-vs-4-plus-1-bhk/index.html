3+1 BHK vs 4+1 BHK: Which Configuration Fits You?

What the "+1" actually adds, and how to decide between the two at Atlantis Three Sixty, PR-7, Zirakpur.

Atlantis Three Sixty · Configuration Guide

If you've been flat-hunting in Zirakpur, you've probably seen listings for "3+1 BHK" or "4+1 BHK" and wondered what that extra "+1" actually adds. It's one of the most commonly searched — and most commonly misunderstood — terms in Indian real estate. At Atlantis Three Sixty on PR-7, Zirakpur, both configurations are available, so here's a clear breakdown of what each one means and which might fit your household better.

What Does "+1" Actually Mean?

In most Indian residential projects, the "+1" refers to an additional utility room — typically a servant room, sometimes used instead as a study, storeroom, or home office. It's not a full bedroom in the traditional sense, but it's built-in flexible space that a standard 3 BHK or 4 BHK doesn't include. The core layout (3 or 4 main bedrooms, living/dining, kitchen) stays the same — the "+1" is what's added on top.

3+1 BHK at Atlantis Three Sixty — Who It's For

Available in Towers A and E, the 3+1 BHK residences run approximately 2,600–2,660 sq. ft., with a servant room, wrap-around balconies open on three sides, and — in select units — a private lift lobby opening directly into the drawing room. This configuration suits families who want the extra utility room for domestic help or a dedicated study, without stepping up to a full fourth bedroom's worth of space and cost.

4+1 BHK at Atlantis Three Sixty — Who It's For

Tower D houses the project's 4+1 BHK residences, at 2,900+ sq. ft. across Stilt + 22 floors. This is the configuration for larger families, multi-generational households, or anyone who wants a dedicated fourth bedroom in addition to the utility/servant room — the largest non-penthouse layout in the project.

Penthouses: 4+1 BHK at Its Largest

For buyers who want the top end of the project, Towers A and E each include two penthouses at approximately 5,320 sq. ft. — still a 4+1 BHK configuration, but at a scale closer to a private residence than a typical apartment.

How to Decide

  • Do you need a dedicated room for domestic help, or a home office/study? Either configuration gives you that — the real decision is 3 vs. 4 main bedrooms.
  • How many people will live in the home day-to-day? A 4+1 suits larger or multi-generational families; a 3+1 is usually enough for a couple with one or two children.
  • What's your budget ceiling? More floor area means a higher starting price — worth weighing against how much of that extra space you'll actually use.

The Bottom Line

Both configurations at Atlantis Three Sixty share the same core design principles — three-side-open residences, wrap-around balconies, and only two homes per floor — so you're not trading away the project's low-density character either way. The choice comes down to household size and how you'd use that extra room.
